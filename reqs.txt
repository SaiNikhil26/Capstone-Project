Intelligent University Course Finder – AI-Powered Multimodal 
Course Discovery System 
Overview 
Universities offer a wide range of courses across multiple departments, disciplines, and 
specialization tracks. While this diversity provides students with many learning opportunities, it 
also creates challenges when students try to identify courses that best match their interests, 
background knowledge, and future career goals. 
Students often describe their learning interests in natural language, focusing on topics or concepts 
rather than specific course names. For example: 
• “I’m interested in learning how computers analyze large datasets and automatically make 
predictions.” 
• “I want to understand how financial markets, investments, and global economic systems 
work.” 
• “As a beginner, I want to learn how machines can perform tasks that normally require 
human intelligence.” 
In addition to text queries, students may also provide information in different forms such as voice 
questions, screenshots of course descriptions, or uploaded documents describing academic 
interests. Traditional course catalogs typically rely on keyword searches and manual browsing, 
which may not capture the true meaning or intent behind these inputs. 
Problem 
Simply retrieving courses based on keywords is often insufficient for effective academic planning. 
Students face several challenges when exploring large course catalogs, including: 
• Understanding prerequisite relationships between courses 
• Identifying knowledge gaps before enrolling in advanced subjects 
• Discovering structured learning paths that connect multiple courses 
• Exploring courses that align with specific career goals or skill areas 
• Interpreting course descriptions that may use technical or domain-specific language 
Additionally, universities increasingly provide course information across different formats such as 
catalog descriptions, program guides, and advising documents, which makes it difficult for 
students to identify the most relevant courses using traditional search systems. 
Objective 
The objective of this project is to build an AI-powered multimodal course discovery and 
recommendation system that helps students explore university course offerings using natural and 
flexible inputs. 
The system will understand student queries expressed through text, voice, or other contextual 
inputs, interpret their learning intent, and retrieve courses that are most relevant to their interests. 
Beyond simple search, the system will also act as an intelligent academic assistant, helping 
students better understand course relationships and make informed decisions about their 
academic path. 
 
Key Capabilities 
 
Multimodal Query Understanding 
Students can interact with the system using different types of inputs such as text queries, voice 
questions, or uploaded descriptions of their interests. The system interprets these inputs to 
understand the student’s learning intent. 
Semantic Course Retrieval 
Instead of relying on keyword matching, the system identifies courses that are conceptually related 
to the student’s query, enabling more meaningful course discovery. 
Relevance Assistance 
The platform helps students understand why certain courses are recommended by highlighting 
relevant topics, concepts, or skills that match the student’s interests. 
Prerequisite Awareness 
The system analyzes course requirements and helps students determine whether they have the 
necessary background knowledge before enrolling in advanced courses. 
Skill Gap Identification 
Students receive suggestions for foundational courses if the system detects that certain 
prerequisite knowledge may be missing. 
Learning Path Recommendations 
The system can suggest structured learning paths that guide students from introductory courses to 
advanced specialization areas. 
Career-Oriented Course Exploration 
Students can explore courses related to potential career paths such as artificial intelligence, data 
science, finance, or software engineering.
